Who it's for

One platform, three ways in

Patients, doctors and radiology specialists each get their own view — with the same case moving between them.

Patients

Upload your knee X-ray, answer a few questions, and wait for a doctor's report in words you understand.

  • Upload in under a minute
  • A simplified, jargon-free report
  • Your full case history in one place

Doctors

Open a queue that is already sorted by urgency, confirm or change the grade, and sign the report.

  • Cases ranked by priority
  • The patient's history beside the image
  • The final grade is always yours

Radiology specialists

Link an X-ray to a registered patient by national ID and send it straight into the review queue.

  • Find a patient by national ID
  • Upload on the patient's behalf
  • Follow the case to its report
How it works

From an X-ray to a report you can read

Four steps. The last one is a person, and that is the point.

1

Upload the X-ray

The patient uploads it, or a radiology specialist links it to the patient's account.

2

Answer a short form

Pain, stiffness, medications and warning signs — the context the doctor needs.

3

The model grades it

A KL grade from 0 to 4 with a confidence score, and a priority for the queue.

4

A doctor signs it

They confirm or change the grade, add notes, and only then does the report reach you.

Our initiative

Built as a public service, not a product

MUFSAL is a university graduation project made for the people who wait longest for an answer. There is no fee, no subscription and nothing to sell — the point is to shorten the distance between a knee that hurts and a doctor who can explain why.
