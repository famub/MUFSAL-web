What happens next

Your complaint does not sit in an inbox — it goes straight to the admin queue.

1 It reaches the admin

The MUFSAL admin sees it the moment you send it.
2 They act on it

A late case can be moved to another available doctor; a report issue goes back for a second look.
3 You get an answer

The reply appears under the complaint on this page.

In a hurry? A complaint is not emergency care. If your knee is badly swollen, locked, or you cannot put weight on it, go to the nearest emergency department.
